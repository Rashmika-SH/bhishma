What is a Kundli Reading?

A Kundli, or Vedic birth chart, is a sacred map of the sky at the exact moment of your birth. It reveals the positions of all nine planets across the 12 houses of your life — each governing a different aspect of your existence from career and wealth to love, health, and spirituality.

Master Psychic Bhishma uses the classical Parashari and Jaimini systems of Jyotish to analyze your Kundli with extraordinary precision. He examines planetary conjunctions, aspects, dashas, and transits to provide a comprehensive picture of your past karma and future destiny.

A Kundli reading is the most detailed and accurate form of astrological guidance available. It gives you a complete roadmap of your life — helping you make informed decisions, avoid pitfalls, and align with your highest cosmic potential.
